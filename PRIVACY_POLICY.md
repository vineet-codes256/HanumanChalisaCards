# Privacy Policy for Hanuman Chalisa Cards

**Last Updated: January 7, 2026**

## Introduction

Hanuman Chalisa Cards ("we", "our", or "the app") is a spiritual devotional application that provides users with the complete Hanuman Chalisa in an interactive card format.

## Information Collection and Use

### Data We Collect

**None.** This app does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information or user data.

### What the App Does:

- Displays the Hanuman Chalisa verses locally on your device
- Plays audio files (bell sounds) stored locally within the app
- All content is accessed offline after installation
- No user accounts, login, or registration required
- No analytics or tracking of any kind

### Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

- **Internet Access**: Only used to open the Google Play Store when users choose to rate the app. No data is transmitted to our servers (we don't have any).

## Third-Party Services

### Google Play Store

When users tap "Rate on Play Store", they are directed to the Google Play Store app or website. This action is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not ours.

No user data is shared with Google or any third party through this app.

## Data Security

Since we don't collect any data, there is no user data to secure or protect. All app content is stored locally on your device.

## Children's Privacy

This app is safe for all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
