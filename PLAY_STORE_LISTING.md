## Full Description (4000 characters max)

Experience the divine Hanuman Chalisa in a beautiful, modern card format! 🙏

**Hanuman Chalisa Cards** brings you the complete 40 verses of the sacred Hanuman Chalisa, presented in an elegant, swipe-through card interface. Perfect for daily devotion, meditation, or learning the Chalisa.

### ✨ Key Features:

📿 **Complete Hanuman Chalisa**
• All 40 Chaupais plus opening and closing Dohas
• Original Hindi (Devanagari) text
• Roman transliteration for easy reading
• Verse-by-verse navigation

🎨 **Beautiful Design**
• Serene mountain background
• Golden and saffron color theme
• Smooth card transitions
• High-quality Hanuman icon

🔔 **Devotional Audio**
• Big bell rings on app launch
• Small bell on Chalisa completion
• Ring the bell button for added devotion

📱 **Modern Features**
• Swipe or tap to navigate verses
• Toggle between Hindi and transliteration
• Progress indicator
• Completion screen with blessings

🌙 **Offline & Private**
• Works completely offline
• No login required
• No data collection
• No ads, completely free

### Why Hanuman Chalisa?

The Hanuman Chalisa is a 40-verse devotional hymn composed by Tulsidas in praise of Lord Hanuman. Reciting it brings:
• Inner strength and courage
• Protection from negative energies
• Mental peace and clarity
• Success in endeavors
• Divine blessings
