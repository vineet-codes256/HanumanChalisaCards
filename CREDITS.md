## 🔔 Audio Assets

### Bell Sounds (Private)
The bell audio files (`BigBell.mp3` and `small-pooja-bell-fast.mp3`) are **not included in this public repository** due to licensing restrictions.

**For contributors/developers:**
- These files are required for the app to function with audio
- If you fork this project, you'll need to source your own bell sound effects
- Suggested open-source alternatives:
  - [Freesound.org](https://freesound.org) - Search for "temple bell" or "pooja bell"
  - [OpenGameArt.org](https://opengameart.org) - Look for bell sound effects
  - Record your own temple bell sounds
  
**File locations (not tracked in git):**
```
public/assets/BigBell.mp3              # Big temple bell (app launch)
public/assets/small-pooja-bell-fast.mp3 # Small pooja bell (completion)
```
