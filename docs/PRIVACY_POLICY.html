Introduction

Hanuman Chalisa Cards ("we", "our", or "the app") is a spiritual devotional application that provides users with the complete Hanuman Chalisa in an interactive card format.

Information Collection and Use

Data We Collect

None. This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or user data.

What the App Does

  • No user accounts, login, or registration required
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind
